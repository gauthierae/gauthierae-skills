# Why my notes app forgets me

Since three years, I write every idea in one notes app. Actually I have 4,000 notes. I assisted to a conference on memory last spring, and the speaker made a resume of the research in ten minutes.

The research is clear. A note that you never read again is a dead note. Eventually, it is worse than no note, because it gives you the feeling that you know.

So I did what every reasonable person does: I bought a second notes app. Problem solved, obviously.

The real fix is a review habit. Each week, I open ten old notes and I ask one question: does this still teach me something? If not, the note goes to the archive.
